## Our Guiding Philosophy:
- Consistency > anything else
- If you can meet a few basic criterias, you will end up unlocking the best in your career:
  - **Show Up**–in every meeting,
  - **Be Active**–on the server with other people,
  - **Learn**–something new everyday,
  - **Apply**–contribute from what you learned

- Although our projects are intimidating, **we leave none of our members behind**! If you'll join us and be willing, greatness will be inevitable!
  - We are a community driven by growth of every individual, you'll have to experience it for yourself.
- Competitive Team Spirit: Our goal is to compete in the prestigious international rocketry competitions:
  - IREC
  - NASA Student Launch
